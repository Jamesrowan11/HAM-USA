About us

Harborline is a small investment company owned by the people who run it. We put our own money to work, and we stay with the companies we back.

We started Harborline Asset Management to do two things well: hold shares in good public companies for the long run, and back tiny companies early, when a modest amount of money and real help can make a difference.

We also own operating businesses. Northvale Supply LLC and its company Rowan Copy are part of the Harborline group, and we help run them day to day.

Our approach

Three ways we invest

Every investment is made with Harborline's own capital and held with a long view.

Public stocks

Long-term positions in listed companies whose businesses we understand and whose prices we think are fair.

  • Businesses we can explain in a sentence
  • Bought to hold for years
  • Sized so no single position can sink us

Early-stage companies

Small investments in tiny companies, often before anyone else has backed them, where our capital and help matter most.

  • Founders who are already building
  • Real customers, or a clear path to them
  • Hands-on support after we invest

Companies we own

Businesses Harborline owns outright or controls, which we help run and grow alongside their teams.

  • Majority or full ownership
  • Shared operations and know-how
  • Profits reinvested across the group

For founders

Building something small that could grow?

We like to meet companies early. Tell us what you're building, and someone on our team will read it.

  • Tiny is fine. Side projects that already have customers are welcome.

  • You're already building. A product, a pilot, or first sales, not just an idea.

  • A business we can understand. Clear customers and a clear way to earn money.

  • Room for help. Beyond capital, we help with the practical work: websites, operations and first customers.

We read every submission, but we can't reply to all of them. If it's a fit, we'll reach out to set up a call.
